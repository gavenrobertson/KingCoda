The realm is a grid of strings: `'.'` is open land, `'#'` is a mountain, `'S'` is the castle where a courier starts and `'E'` is the fort they must reach.

Each step moves the courier one square **up, down, left or right** onto open land (or onto `'E'`). Return the fewest steps needed to reach the fort, or `-1` if it can't be reached.
